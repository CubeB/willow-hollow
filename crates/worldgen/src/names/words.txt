# Words that place names are made from. Data, not code: lists can be extended, and new
# tongues added, without touching the logic in names/mod.rs.
#
# Each line is `key = values`. Values are separated by `|` where a line has any, else by
# spaces. Repeating a value makes it likelier. Everything after `#` is a comment.
#
# Patterns fill these slots:
#   {root}   a word of the region's tongue, such as Haldvik    {low}  the same, lower case
#   {stem}   just the first part of it, such as Hald
#   {word}   a word for the surroundings, from [feature]: Bog, Goldtree, Willow
#   {role}   what the place does, from its kind: watch, rest, hold
#   {type}   what the place is, from its kind: Outlook, Ruin, Shrine
#   {adj}    a word of age or character, from its kind: Old, Lone, Quiet
#   {near}   the name of a natural feature it stands by: Greymere, Haldvik
#   {owner}  someone who lives there, or a name from the tongue for places people keep
# A slot written straight against letters or another slot ("{word}{role}", "{word}mere")
# makes a compound, and only short single words are put into those.
